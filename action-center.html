🔔 Central Notification / Action Center

⌂ ERP Dashboard 📋 Audit Trail

One control tower for every open item across the ERP

Every module (Employee, CICPA, Leave, Client, Subcontractor, Timesheet, Payroll, Site) feeds into this single list. Click any item's action button to open the exact record. Nothing is just dismissed — each item follows New → Acknowledged → In Progress → Resolved, and resolves itself automatically once the underlying issue is fixed.

Role filters limit what each team sees so nobody has to scan through 100 irrelevant alerts. "All (Admin)" shows everything. Resolving an item records who resolved it and when — visible in Audit Trail as well, since every status change is a normal localStorage write.
